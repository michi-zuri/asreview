WHY
  Part 4's client compares row versions with the change feed. Every row
  must therefore carry its version, and every list response must carry
  the project version (`watermark`) read BEFORE the rows. /labeled reads
  results through sqlite3 and records through the SQLAlchemy DataStore
  (two connections, two snapshots). Reading the watermark first means a
  concurrent write can only make the rows NEWER than the watermark. The
  next poll then re-checks harmlessly and never misses a change (D11).
