WHY
  Today every edit and every PDF found by the live check calls
  invalidateQueries(["fetchLabeledRecord", …]), which refetches EVERY
  loaded page (several MB per minute while scrolling a "Full text: false"
  view). The card key embeds JSON of the note, the tags_form, the
  lists_form and the lists, so every change remounts the card (an open
  abstract collapses). After this task:
    - /labeled is requested only on mount, on filter/subset change and
      when scrolling (fetchNextPage).
    - An own edit replaces just that row in the cached pages.
    - A PDF found by the live check updates only that card's attachment
      display.
    - Records never vanish by themselves (D1). Membership changes show
      after a reload until Part 4 adds the banner.
